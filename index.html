E-Commerce Sales Analyst

Dashboard Development

Technologies

SQL, Power BI

Problem

An e-commerce company needed better visibility into its business performance. The leadership team lacked a centralized reporting tool to monitor sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, and regional performance.

Solution

I analyzed the e-commerce dataset using SQL and Power BI to explore sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, and regional performance. The data was cleaned and transformed using Power Query, and relationships between tables were established to build a structured data model. Key business metrics were calculated using DAX, and the results were presented in an interactive Power BI dashboard to support data-driven decision making.

Result

The final dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance and allows stakeholders to: Track sales growth over time, identify top-performing products, analyze customer purchasing patterns, compare regional performance. The dashboard enables faster data-driven decision making and provides leadership with clear insights into the company's performance.

Student Habits and Academic Performance Analysis

Dashboard Development

Technologies

SQL, Power BI

Problem

Academic performance is influenced by many factors beyond intelligence, including study habits, lifestyle, and digital distractions. However, it is often unclear which factors have the strongest impact on students' exam scores. The goal of this project is to answer the main business question: “What factors most influence students' academic performance?”

Solution

SQL was used to explore and analyze the dataset by examining relationships between exam scores and various student behaviors, including study hours, attendance, sleep habits, exercise, social media usage, and other lifestyle factors. The results were then visualized in Power BI dashboards to highlight key patterns and correlations between student habits and academic performance.

Result

Provided clear insights into the key factors influencing student performance, identifying study habits, sleep patterns, and digital distractions as major drivers of exam scores. The analysis enabled a data-driven understanding of student success factors through interactive Power BI dashboards.

Coffee Sales Performance Dashboard

Dashboard Development

Technologies

Excel Pivot Tables, Calculated Fields, Slicers, Charts

Problem

Lack of clear visibility into coffee sales performance across product types, roast levels, countries, and customer segments. Without consolidated insights, it was difficult to identify best-selling products, high-value markets, top customers, or seasonal trends, hindering data-driven decisions and growth strategies.

Solution

Built an interactive Excel dashboard using Pivot Tables, calculated fields, slicers, and multiple charts. The dashboard integrates Orders, Customers, and Products data to analyze total sales, monthly/yearly trends by coffee type (Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, Robusta), roast type (Light/Medium/Dark), country performance (US, Ireland, UK), top 5 customers, and loyalty card impact — all with one-click filtering by year, month, and product attributes.

Result

Delivered immediate, actionable insights: United States contributes ~78% of total revenue (35,638 USD), top-performing coffee types and roast levels were clearly identified, and top 5 customers generated significant value. Enabled faster decision-making on inventory, marketing focus, and customer retention, resulting in improved sales efficiency and a professional tool ready for business use.
